Texas Emergency Preparedness Protocol

Hurricanes & Tropical Storms

  • Maintain an emergency supply kit (food/water for 7 days).
  • Know your Texas Coastal Evacuation Route.
  • Secure windows and fuel all family vehicles.

Tornadoes & Severe Storms

  • Move to an interior room without windows on the lowest floor.
  • Keep SAVTEX notifications enabled on mobile devices.
  • Have helmets and sturdy shoes accessible.

Winter Freeze & Grid Resilience

  • Insulate outdoor pipes and drain irrigation lines.
  • Maintain non-electric heating sources safely.
  • Store extra blankets and thermal gear.
